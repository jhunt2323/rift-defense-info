How do I play?

Aliens come out of the rift and follow the road to your base. Before each wave, pick a card: a road piece, a free tower, or an upgrade. Tap a tower to upgrade it with credits. Win every wave to finish a level and unlock a new tower. The in-game How to Play screen has more.

My progress didn't carry over to my other device

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that "Save progress to iCloud" is on in the game's Game Center screen. Open the game on each device while online.

How do I restore my purchases?

On the tower picking screen, open the power-ups and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

How do I sign out of Game Center?

Apple only allows this from the iPhone Settings app: Settings › Game Center.

How do I start over?

Home screen › Settings › Reset all progress. Your purchases are kept.
