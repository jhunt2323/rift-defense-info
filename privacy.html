Privacy Policy

Rift Defense, by Joseph Huntley / CapableProcess.ai. Effective October 26, 2026.

The short version

Rift Defense does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of our own.

Your game progress

Your level, unlocked towers, points, research and settings are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account so it carries over to your other Apple devices. We cannot see or access this data; it is stored by Apple under your Apple Account.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple handles your sign-in and profile. The game only uses it to recognise that you are signed in. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The optional power-ups (Nuclear Strike and Power Boost) are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@capableprocess.ai.
